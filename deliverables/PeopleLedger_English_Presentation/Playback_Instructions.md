# PeopleLedger English presentation

20 slides with embedded English narration. Total automatic playback: 19 minutes 11 seconds.

## Files

- `PeopleLedger_English_Narrated.pptx`: editable slides. Open in desktop PowerPoint and press F5 to start.
- `PeopleLedger_English_AutoPlay.ppsx`: opens as a slideshow in desktop PowerPoint.
- `PeopleLedger_English_Narration.md`: the complete narration for reading and rehearsal.

Both PowerPoint files include the audio. No separate audio folder is required. Slides advance 1.5 seconds after each narration ends. Keep PowerPoint's narration and slide timing settings enabled. The slide notes contain the English script.

## Online presentation

Click Start narration to enable sound in the browser. Use Pause, the contents list, or the navigation arrows as needed. Browser playback requires an internet connection.
